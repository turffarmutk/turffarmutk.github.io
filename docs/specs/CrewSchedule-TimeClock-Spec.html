1. What this module does

Undergrads don't all work every day, so Bill needs to know who's coming in and when before he hands out the day's tasks. This module replaces the availability whiteboard: each hourly worker enters their available days and times, logs time off, and can flag a same-day absence. Separately, a time clock records actual punch in/out and totals weekly hours so Bill can cross-check payroll. Together they give Bill a live forward roster that feeds task assignment.

3. Availability schedule

The core replacement for the whiteboard, in two layers:

Usual weekly pattern — set each semester

Each worker sets their standing availability — for each weekday, available or not, with a time window (e.g. "Mon 7am–1pm, Wed 8am–12pm, Fri off"). This is what they'd normally write on the board.

Because class schedules change, patterns are tied to a semester — Fall, Winter, and Summer — and must be re-entered each term. At the start of a new term the app prompts each worker to enter their new pattern, and the prior term's pattern is archived rather than carried over.

Exceptions — vacations & conflicts

Any specific date can override the pattern — an extra day, different hours, or a day off. This is where workers enter vacations and one-off conflicts with their usual pattern (a class event, an appointment, a trip). Exceptions win over the standing pattern for that date.

Entry is self-service; every add or change posts an in-app notice to Bill (no push). Bill sees a combined week/day roster of who's available and when, and a "who's in today" summary that drives task assignment.

4. Time off & same-day call-outs

Time off / unavailability calendar

Workers log planned absences over a date range (vacation, travel, exams). These show on a calendar so Bill sees gaps well ahead and can plan around them.

24-hour cutoff — short notice needs Bill's approval

How a time-off request comes off the schedule depends on how close the shift is:

  • More than 24 hours out → self-service. The shift comes off automatically and Bill gets an in-app notice.
  • Within 24 hours of the shift start → it becomes a request Bill must approve. The worker stays on the schedule until Bill approves; on approval the shift is removed. Bill sees pending requests to approve or deny.

Same-day call-out

A quick "I can't come in today" action for sickness or life events. Because it's within 24 hours, it follows the cutoff above — it posts an in-app notice to Bill (no push) and stays on the schedule as a pending request until Bill approves the removal. It's logged the instant it's sent, but it only excuses the day once Bill approves it — an unapproved absence with no clock-in that day becomes a no-show (§5). Workers are still expected to contact Bill directly as well — the call-out is the record, not a replacement for that heads-up.

5. No-show tracking (Bill only)

A day is a NO-SHOW when a scheduled worker never clocks in that day and has no approved absence for it. Bill's approval is the deciding switch:

  • Bill approves the absence → the day is excused and any no-show for it is wiped.
  • Absence not approved (denied or never approved) and no clock-in at any point that day → it stands as a no-show.

Each no-show increments a running count per worker that only Bill sees — a simple accountability tally. The clock-in data does the detecting; Bill's approve/deny decides whether it's excused, so he stays in control.

6. Time clock

Informal presence tracking, not the official payroll record — it gives Bill clean timestamps to cross-check when approving payroll.

  • Who: undergrad hourly + future hourly (role-based, so new hourly staff get it automatically).
  • Clock in / out: one big button on arrival and departure. Location-gated to a 0.5-mile geofence around the farm (35.90203° N, 83.95762° W). A worker can punch multiple times a day — out for lunch, back in — and every in/out pair counts toward the day's hours.
  • Captures: clock-in, clock-out, date, person, and whether the location was verified.
  • Bill's view: the punch log per person plus an auto-summed weekly (Mon–Fri) hours total, side-by-side-ready with the payroll timesheet. Location-unverified punches are flagged for him to confirm.
  • Worker's view: each worker sees their own weekly hours total (not anyone else's).
  • Corrections: Bill can edit or add a missed punch and annotate it.
  • Not doing (v1): calculating pay, enforcing breaks, or acting as the legal timekeeping record.

A no-show is exactly the gap between the two: expected on the schedule, no clock-in all day, and no approved absence.

8. Screens (v1)

  1. My schedule (worker) — set weekly availability + times, add time off, and the same-day call-out button; see their own upcoming days and their own weekly hours total.
  2. Crew roster (Bill + techs/grads) — week/day grid of who's available and when, the time-off calendar, and "who's in today." Bill also sees and acts on pending short-notice (≤24h) requests to approve or deny. This is one shared calendar component with a toggle to overlay the Studies layer (see Trial Protocols & Study Plans), so Bill can view crew availability and research together or separately.
  3. Clock in / out (worker) — the big location-gated button.
  4. Time & hours (Bill) — punch log per person, weekly hours totals, edit/annotate, mark no-show, and the per-worker no-show count. Bill only.

9. Notifications

  • Schedule add/change → in-app notice to Bill.
  • Short-notice (≤24h) request or same-day call-out → in-app notice to Bill, flagged as awaiting his approval.

No push anywhere in this module — consistent with the app's minimal-push approach (the only push is "task assigned to you").

Weekly hours totals are derived from time_punch. A no-show is rule-based — a scheduled day with no clock-in and no approved absence — so a no_show row is created when that holds at day's end and cleared if Bill later approves the absence. The count is the worker's standing no_show rows.

11. Out of scope for v1 (comes later)

  • Payroll calculation or export to a payroll system (this only informs Bill's approval)
  • Break tracking and legal timekeeping compliance
  • Shift swapping / approval workflows
  • Techs, grads, and faculty entering their own availability (v1 is hourly-focused)

12. Decisions & open items

Resolved:

  • Clock-in geofence: centered on the farm at 35.90203° N, 83.95762° W (3450 Maloney Rd, Knoxville, TN), 0.5-mile radius. Inside → passes; outside → blocked.
  • If location is unavailable (permission denied, location services off, airplane mode, or poor signal): the punch is allowed but flagged "location unverified" for Bill to confirm — no hard block over a phone glitch.
  • Multiple punches per day: allowed — clock out and back in (lunch, leaving and returning); each in/out pair is its own punch and all count toward the day's hours.
  • Payroll week: Monday–Friday for the weekly hours total.
  • Semester start dates: Fall begins Aug 1, Winter Jan 1, Summer Jun 1 — each triggers the availability re-entry prompt.
When is location "unavailable"? The phone can fail to return a position if the worker denies the location permission, has location services off, is in airplane mode, or is somewhere with poor signal (inside a metal barn) so the fix times out or is too inaccurate. A 0.5-mile radius is generous, so genuine on-site punches almost always pass; the "unverified, flag for Bill" path is the safety valve for the rest.
