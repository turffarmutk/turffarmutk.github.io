UT Turf Farm — Plot Splitter

Cut plots into a / b / c
Base Plot Pick a plot to split.
Pick a plot, click Draw cut, then click two points across it to slice. Add more cuts for 3 pieces. Match the piece areas to the targets, Reverse a/b/c if the lettering is flipped, then Apply split. When all are done, Export plots to save the updated set. Tap a piece to rename its letter.
